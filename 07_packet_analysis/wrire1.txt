# Wireshark — quick refresher

## Purpose
Wireshark captures and analyses packets on supported platforms, including Linux and Windows. It can inspect live traffic or saved captures such as pcap and pcapng.

## Workflow
1. Choose the interface that carries the traffic.
2. Apply a capture filter when collecting; use a display filter while analysing.
3. Inspect protocol fields, timing, retransmissions and stream relationships.
4. Correlate observations with host and NIC counters.

## Filters
Capture filter example: udp port 319
Display filter example: udp.port == 319
TCP analysis example: tcp.analysis.retransmission

Capture and display filters use different syntaxes. A display filter does not remove packets from the saved capture.

## Limits
Wireshark is primarily a protocol analyser, not a standalone IDS or packet generator. Colour rules aid reading; they are not proof of a fault. Capture drops and timestamp quality affect conclusions. Ordinary OS captures may miss kernel-bypass traffic.
