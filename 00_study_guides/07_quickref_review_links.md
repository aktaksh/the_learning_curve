## 4 Five minute revision routine

Choose one sheet, recall three operations before opening it, then check the syntax. Practise one small example in a lab and explain the result. For HFT study, connect the command to the question you are trying to answer rather than treating a cheat sheet as a tuning recipe.

These are third-party online resources and require internet access. They are links, not copied or independently certified course content. Validate version-specific details against the tool's official documentation before operational use.
