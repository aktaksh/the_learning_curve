## Finding material

The HTML reader searches titles, paths and complete text. Topic groups distinguish CCNA, CCNP, personal networking notes, C++, Python lessons and exercises, automation, Linux performance and English. Each article has its own section index. PDF books remain linked reference resources; their full contents are not part of text search.

## Content processing scope

Cleaned articles are in `Study_Guides`. The original notes and current working edits remain in their original locations. Empty placeholders and compiled binaries are excluded from article search. Similar CCNA note variants remain separately accessible because they are not exact duplicates. Existing course chapters are indexed as supplied rather than presented as independently fact-checked material.

Personal networking notes now provide concise refreshers for CCNA fundamentals, Ethernet, PTP and STP. DPDK notes cover queues, packet layout and the l2fwd example. The HFT syllabus placeholder and Go directory still contain no study material.
